Hand-curated, 2026-09-18 — same "can't be scraped like the S&P 500/Nasdaq-100
snapshots" reasoning as crypto_universe.csv/forex_universe.csv (see their own
.meta.txt files): there's no single canonical "semiconductor index"
constituent list to pull the way there is for a broad-market index.

Built to test a real hypothesis, not as a general-purpose universe: watching
eToro Popular Investors Aukie2008 and RainbirdFx (see copy_trading.py) showed
both heavily concentrated in semiconductors specifically — worth checking
whether Chopper's existing minute-bar strategies (VWAP Mean Reversion,
Bollinger Mean Reversion, Linear Regression Channel) perform differently on
a semiconductor-heavy ticker list than the current S&P 500/Nasdaq-100 scan.

27 major, liquid, US-exchange-listed semiconductor design/manufacturing/
equipment companies (large caps down to some mid-caps) — deliberately
excludes non-US-listed names we can't execute through Alpaca/IBKR (e.g. the
Euronext Amsterdam-listed ASM International, ASM.NV, which Aukie2008 itself
trades — ASML is included here via its US ADR, ASM International is not).
Sector/sub_industry columns use GICS-style labels to match sp500_constituents
.csv's own taxonomy, so sector_for_ticker() and anything else that reads
across universe CSVs stays consistent.
